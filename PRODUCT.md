## Platform

## Users

Recruiters at companies and people evaluating Jeffrey Verdú's professional experience and technical knowledge.

## Product Purpose

A professional portfolio that makes Jeffrey's Full Stack background, experience, technical skills and projects easy to understand and provides access to his CV and contact details.

## Positioning

Jeffrey works on a production, Docker-based Ruby on Rails platform alongside an official administrative and financial role at Academia Desafío Latam. His background also includes React projects and Full Stack JavaScript/PERN training. Present the official role and development responsibilities accurately.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing React, TypeScript and Vite application; Spanish content.
- Preserve factual content from the supplied 2026 CV; do not invent impact metrics, employers, projects or seniority.
- Downloadable CV, project previews and existing project URLs, LinkedIn and email contact.
- The user authorizes a complete redesign and does not require preservation of any visual element, photo or logo.
- Publishing is separate from the current local redesign.

## Brand Commitments

Professional, contemporary and novel without being pretentious. Voice: clear, direct Spanish, with concrete descriptions of work.

## Evidence on Hand

- `public/CV_Jeffrey_Verdu_Full_Stack_Developer_2026.pdf`: supplied by the user, current factual source.
- `public/projects.json`: two projects, PosicionAR! and LSV Service, LLC, with existing public URLs.
- `public/portada-posicionar.png` and `public/portada-lsvservicellc.png`: existing project screenshots.
- Existing personal photos and logo are available but optional.
- No testimonials, quantitative business results, or public screenshots of the employer platform have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make professional fit understandable in a short first visit.
- Let actual work and accurate experience support technical claims.
- Keep the CV and contact accessible throughout the experience.
- Use novelty to improve exploration, never to obscure content.
